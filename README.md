# Nissan Bank Application Form

Fill out a client's bank application on your computer, phone or tablet, let them sign on the screen, and save it. Each saved application goes into the **Bank Applications** Google Sheet with a PDF copy.

**Open the form:** https://kevinsornet.github.io/Online-Bank-Application-form/

---

## For marketing professionals: filling out the form

### Start a new application
1. Open the form link above.
2. If someone else's details are still on the form, click **Clear** first.
3. At the top, pick your **Dealer branch** (Alabang, Calamba, Carmona, Dasmariñas, Sta. Rosa or Sucat). The form header changes to match. The form remembers your branch next time.

### Fill in the details
- Type into the boxes on the left. The **preview** on the right shows the real form filling in as you type. What you see there is what gets saved and printed.
- **Birthday:** pick the date and the age fills in by itself.
- **Prices and income:** type numbers only, like `1250000`. The form adds the ₱ sign and commas.
- **Residence:** choose Owned, Used free or Renting.
- **ALL CAPS** (top of the page) prints everything in capital letters except email addresses. Untick it if you want normal letters.
- Leave anything blank that doesn't apply, such as extra co-makers or references.
- Your typing is kept on this device, so if you close the page by accident, it will still be there when you come back.

### Signatures
1. Scroll to **E-signatures** at the bottom of the form.
2. Hand the device to each person to sign their pad with a finger, stylus, mouse or trackpad:
   - **Nissan marketing professional**: you
   - **Group retail manager**
   - **Client's signature**
3. Made a mistake? Click **Clear** under that pad and sign again.

The names printed under the signature lines come from the **Signature names** section. If you leave those blank, the form uses your name and the applicant's name.

### Save it
Click **Save to Sheet** at the top. It says *Making PDF…*, then *Saving…*, then **Saved to sheet (row __)**.

- Changed something after saving? Just click **Save to Sheet** again. It updates the same application. It does **not** make a duplicate.
- If you see **Save failed**, check your internet connection and try again. Your details stay on the form, so nothing is lost.

### Print or download a copy
Click **Print / Save PDF**. In the print window:
- To print: pick your printer and set the paper size to **Legal**.
- To get a file: pick **Save as PDF**. The file is named after the applicant.

### Next client
Click **Clear** and confirm. This empties the form and signatures. The next save becomes a new application. Your branch stays selected.

> **Privacy:** these forms hold clients' TIN, birthday and mother's maiden name. Only share the form link with the sales team, and click **Clear** when you're done on a shared or borrowed device.

## For managers and staff: using the sheet

**Open the sheet:** [Bank Applications](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1l38-iRBzpxufuH7n_0PZMVVlYLKATp3fWR4iXdF0P2U/edit). You need to be given access. If you can't open it, ask the sheet owner to share it with you.

### What's in it
- Each **row** is one application. Saving it again updates that same row.
- The first columns are the most useful:
  - **Saved At**: the last time it was saved
  - **Branch**: which dealer branch it came from
  - **Saved PDF**: click to open the filled form as a PDF, ready to print or send to the bank
- The rest of the columns hold each part of the form, grouped by section (Applicant details, Spouse / co-maker, Bank account references, and so on).
- The **Signature** columns at the far right link to each signature image.

PDFs are kept in the Drive folder **Bank Application PDFs**, and signatures in **Bank Application Signatures**.

### Finding an application
- Press **Ctrl + F** (or **⌘ + F** on a Mac) and type the client's name.
- Or use **Data → Create a filter** to filter by Branch, date or marketing professional.
- To sort, use the filter arrows. Don't sort by selecting just one column, or the rows will get mixed up.

### Please don't
- **Rename or delete the column headers** in row 1, or the **Record ID** column. The form uses them to know where to save.
- **Type into rows by hand** to make new applications. Use the form instead, so the PDF and signatures are saved too.
- **Share the sheet or the PDFs** outside the people who need them. They contain clients' personal information.

It's fine to add your own extra columns at the far right, like **Status** or **Bank decision**. The form leaves those alone.
